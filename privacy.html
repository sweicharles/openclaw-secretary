Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Who uses this app. "openclaw secretary" is a personal tool operated by its owner, Charles Swei, for his own use only. No other user accounts exist.

What data it accesses. With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app reads the owner's Gmail messages and Google Calendar events using read-only scopes. It does not send mail, create or change events, or delete anything.

How data is used. Mail and calendar data are read only to answer the owner's own requests, such as summarising recent mail or listing today's events. Data is processed on the owner's own computer and sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen in order to produce those answers.

Sharing. Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party other than the AI model provider named above, and only for the purpose of answering the owner's requests.

Storage and retention. Access tokens are stored in the owner's operating-system keychain on the owner's computer. Mail and calendar content are not stored beyond the conversation in which they were requested.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
